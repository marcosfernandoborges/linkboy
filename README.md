# LINKBOY
### Decentralized Last-Mile Delivery Ecosystem

**Connecting local merchants, independent couriers, and the Solana ecosystem.**

## The Problem

Small businesses often depend on expensive delivery marketplaces or must maintain their own delivery workforce.

Merchants selling through WhatsApp, Instagram, and digital catalogs need an affordable and flexible way to request independent couriers without paying commissions on their product sales.

## Our Solution

LINKBOY is a last-mile delivery platform designed to connect local merchants with nearby independent couriers on demand.

Merchants request a delivery when an order is ready. Available couriers receive the opportunity and can accept or decline it.

The platform aims to provide transparent delivery pricing, efficient dispatching, and independent earning opportunities.

## How It Works

1. A merchant receives an order through their existing sales channel.
2. The merchant requests a courier through LINKBOY.
3. Nearby available couriers receive the delivery request.
4. A courier accepts and collects the order.
5. The delivery is completed and verified.
6. The platform records the transaction and calculates the corresponding economic allocations.

## Business Model

Our initial proposed delivery fee is BRL 14, subject to distance-based pricing.

- BRL 11 — Courier compensation
- BRL 2 — LINKBOY platform revenue
- BRL 1 — Proposed digital asset ecosystem allocation

The digital asset allocation is envisioned to support reserves, courier rewards, and token ecosystem activities, subject to further economic validation and regulatory review.

## Why Solana?

LINKBOY is exploring Solana for its low transaction costs, fast settlement capabilities, and infrastructure for digital assets.

Potential integrations include:

- Token-based courier rewards
- On-chain records of verified delivery activity
- Transparent ecosystem treasury operations
- Digital wallets and programmable incentives

These blockchain features are planned and are not yet represented as deployed functionality.

## Project Status

**Early-stage concept and MVP planning.**

The project is currently defining its technical architecture, logistics workflow, pricing model, and proposed Solana integration.

## Vision

Build an accessible delivery infrastructure where local commerce generates real economic activity and independent couriers can participate in a digital rewards ecosystem.
